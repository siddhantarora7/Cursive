# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who love the act of typing and write in short-to-medium form: students drafting essays, developers writing posts, journaling enthusiasts, Monkeytype/mechanical-keyboard people. Context: a browser tab, a blank page, often recording their screen. Job: get words down and enjoy every keystroke.

## Product Purpose

Cursive is "Monkeytype for writing, with Copilot built in" — the most satisfying place on the internet to type. Inline AI ghost-text completions (Tab to accept), a buttery animated caret, tasteful typing effects, beautiful themes. Local-first, no accounts, free tier + BYOK. Success: clips of it feel so good people share them; writers keep the tab pinned.

## Brand Personality

Fluid, precise, playful-under-restraint. The interface is quiet document chrome; the *feel* (caret, motion, effects) carries the brand. Emotional goal: flow-state satisfaction, never gimmick.

## Anti-references

- Google Docs / Notion feature sprawl — we are not a document suite.
- AI-writing tools that grab the wheel (agent panels, doc rewrites, chat sidebars).
- Gamer-RGB overload — effects are tasteful, individually toggleable, OFF-leaning defaults.

## Design Principles

1. Feel is the product: 60fps and zero added input latency are correctness, not polish.
2. Chrome when you need utility, invisible when in flow (Zen fades everything but text).
3. A bad suggestion is worse than none — silence over noise, in AI and in UI.
4. Demoable by default: every visual decision should look great in a screen recording.
5. Local-first and private: the user's words belong to the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for text in all shipped themes; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables smoothing/effects; Tab never traps when no suggestion is visible; ghost text is aria-hidden with editor behavior unchanged for screen readers; every toolbar control keyboard-operable with visible focus.
